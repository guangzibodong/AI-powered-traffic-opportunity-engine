# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-06-08*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** TrafScope helps businesses discover hidden traffic opportunities and turn them into content, AI citations, and leads.

**What it does:** TrafScope connects ecommerce store data, WordPress pages, Google Search Console signals, and execution history. It finds demand signals, maps them to products and pages, scores the opportunity, generates tasks, creates draft assets, and tracks performance.

**Product category:** AI-powered traffic opportunity engine, AI SEO workflow software, organic growth operations workspace.

**Product type:** B2B SaaS for ecommerce, SEO, content marketing, and agency teams.

**Business model:** Planned subscription SaaS, with agency and multi-store tiers likely.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** WooCommerce and WordPress merchants, SaaS companies, B2B brands, ecommerce teams, independent stores, SEO agencies, and content marketing teams.

**Decision-makers:** Founders, heads of growth, SEO leads, content leads, ecommerce managers, agency owners.

**Primary use case:** Decide which organic traffic actions to take next based on real demand signals and execution readiness.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Turn search and store data into prioritized growth tasks.
- Create content assets that are grounded in product and query evidence.
- Track whether traffic actions improve clicks, impressions, rankings, AI visibility, and leads.

**Use cases:**

- Find high-impression, low-click keywords.
- Build collection pages and buying guides for real search demand.
- Refresh pages that are ranking but underperforming.
- Create WordPress draft pages from approved traffic tasks.
- Give agencies a repeatable traffic planning workflow for client stores.

## Problems And Pain Points

**Core problem:** Teams have search data, products, pages, and content ideas, but they cannot reliably decide which traffic action deserves execution next.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- SEO tools show data but often stop before execution.
- AI writing tools generate content without enough store evidence.
- Manual audits are slow, inconsistent, and hard to repeat.
- Dashboards report performance but do not create next actions.

**What it costs them:** Missed traffic opportunities, slow content production, low-confidence prioritization, duplicate work, and weak follow-through after publishing.

**Emotional tension:** Users know there is demand hidden in their data, but they do not trust another generic AI recommendation unless it explains the evidence.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** SEO platforms and AI SEO tools that recommend content opportunities.

**Secondary:** AI writing tools, keyword tools, content optimization tools, and SEO audit software.

**Indirect:** Manual agency audits, spreadsheets, ad hoc content calendars, and founder intuition.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Traffic operations board instead of analytics-only dashboard.
- Deterministic opportunity scoring before LLM generation.
- Product-query-page graph for ecommerce-specific recommendations.
- Every task includes evidence, related objects, score breakdown, and acceptance criteria.
- WordPress draft-only publishing with human approval.
- Performance loop after execution.

**How we do it differently:** TrafScope starts from signals and maps them into executable tasks. The AI layer helps explain and draft, but the core recommendation logic remains traceable.

**Why that's better:** Users can trust the recommendation, approve the task, create the draft, and measure the result in one workflow.

**Why customers choose us:** They want action and accountability, not another pile of keywords.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| I already have SEO tools. | TrafScope turns signals into tasks and assets, not just reports. |
| I do not trust AI content. | MVP uses deterministic scoring and evidence first; AI drafts are structured and QA checked. |
| I do not want automatic publishing. | MVP is draft-only and requires human approval. |
| My store data is messy. | The graph and readiness scores are designed to expose gaps before execution. |

**Anti-persona:** Teams that only want backlink databases, fully autonomous AI publishing, or broad social scraping before the core search-to-execution loop works.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Keyword lists, audits, and dashboards do not tell teams what to do next.

**Pull:** A focused task board with evidence, scoring, draft generation, and performance tracking.

**Habit:** Existing SEO spreadsheets, content calendars, and manual agency workflows.

**Anxiety:** Users worry AI will invent facts, create low-quality pages, or publish unsafe content.
