# Refero Styles Learning Notes

Reference: https://styles.refero.design/

## What To Learn

The useful lesson from Refero Styles is not a single look. It is the workflow:

1. Choose a reference by screen job and audience, not brand name.
2. Copy the visual system, not the website.
3. Give the AI builder the design context before the first draft.
4. Define colors, type, spacing, components, layout density, and Do/Don't rules.
5. Ask for a concrete screen with acceptance criteria.

## Patterns Worth Applying To TrafScope

- Strong content hierarchy before decoration.
- Clear design tokens for colors, typography, spacing, radius, and components.
- Low-saturation borders and restrained surfaces.
- Compact pill controls for filtering and state.
- Detail pages with a preview/content side and a structured reference side.
- Cards that combine a strong preview area with short, high-signal title and description.
- Do/Don't guidance that prevents generic AI output.

## What Not To Copy Directly

- The visual gallery product model.
- Oversized marketing hero treatment inside the app.
- Large-radius cards in dense product surfaces.
- Brand-specific typography or imagery.
- Decorative screenshots that do not help the merchant make a decision.

## TrafScope Translation

Refero's `DESIGN.md` examples become TrafScope's own `DESIGN.md`:

- Theme: dark operational workspace.
- Product job: prioritize evidence-backed ecommerce traffic tasks.
- Tokens: dark neutrals, green action, blue search evidence, amber commerce evidence.
- Layout: Traffic Operations Board first, detail side rails, structured asset review.
- Components: Task Card, Opportunity Detail, Evidence Row, Score Breakdown, QA Rail.
- Rule: recommendations must be credible before they are beautiful.
